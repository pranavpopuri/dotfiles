\section{Statistics and Calculus}
For a random variable $X$, write $\E[X]$, $\Var(X)$, and $\Prob(X>0)$.
The gradient is $\grad f$ and convergence in probability is $X_n\pto X$.
\begin{theorem}
For every real $x$, $x^2\geq 0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $x\geq 0$, multiply by $x$; otherwise apply the same argument to $-x$.
\end{proof}
\[
\int_0^1\int_0^{1-x}xy\,\dd y\,\dd x=\frac{1}{24},
\qquad
f(x)=\begin{cases}x^2 & x\geq0,\\-x & x<0.\end{cases}
\]
